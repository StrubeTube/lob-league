LOB League · Commissioner's Lab · not league policy

The rule simulator

The 2026 rule set is locked except for one question: how many keepers? Locked: the flattened salary table, keepers count against team salary, keepers slot on the board at their escalated round, and the cap band — $230 cap / $160 floor, fitted to six seasons of replayed history (blocks 15 of the 16 tagged fire sales at the least damage to normal trading; the 16th sold a $10 keeper star for a pile of bodies, which no salary rule can see). Drag the sliders and watch every season re-judge itself; keeper count needs no toggle — 3-or-fewer keeps run budget-free, a 4th or 5th puts the class under the budget. Settings save on this device.

The sliders

Keeper count is the open vote, and the site shows both worlds at once: with 3 or fewer keeps no budget applies (the 3-keeper rules); a 4th or 5th keep puts the whole keeper class under the budget below (the 5-keeper rules). Players preview both from their own Keeper corner.
Team cap
Lower → tighter market: fire-sale hauls impossible to absorb, more cap jail. Higher → looser: easier star-stacking. Fitted value: $230.
Team floor
Higher → fire sales die: sellers must take real salary back. Lower/off → liquidations return. Fitted value: $160. Waivers never trip it.
4+‑keeper budget
Only binds a team keeping 4 or 5 — their whole keeper class must fit under it. A kept R1 star costs $36, so at $50 a star leaves room for bargains only ($36+$10+$3); skipping the star buys a fistful of mids ($14+$12+$10+$8+$6).
