The Trade Archive

Every trade ever, scored by one number per side. Value = position-adjusted points over replacement (rest of season, on your roster — QB points count less because replacement QBs score more) + a 30% keeper premium for producers drafted cheap + pick value from six years of draft history, discounted 15% per year until the pick conveys. A side is FLEECED at a 50+ value deficit.
